# Promo video credits

`extreme-infinitv-promo.mp4` (1920x1080, 30 fps, 33 s) and `extreme-infinitv-promo-square.mp4` (1080x1080) were rendered with Remotion from footage of the app running against a fictional "Northwind IPTV" provider (all channel names, programmes, movies and series are invented; logos and posters are generated placeholders).

All audio is CC0 (public domain), so no attribution is required. Courtesy credits:

- Music: "Unpretentious Reveal (no drums)" by Sondre Drakensson, <https://freesound.org/people/SondreDrakensson/sounds/529381/>, CC0 1.0. Trimmed, faded, level-adjusted.
- Transition: "Whoosh stereo light (transition)" by xkeril, <https://freesound.org/people/xkeril/sounds/701104/>, CC0 1.0.
- Logo hit: "Low impact" by AudioPapkin, <https://freesound.org/people/AudioPapkin/sounds/430978/>, CC0 1.0.
- UI ticks: the app's own `ui-nav.wav` / `ui-select.wav` (`public/sounds`, synthesised in-project).

Fonts: Geist Variable (SIL OFL 1.1) via `@fontsource-variable/geist`. Icons: Tabler Icons (MIT).
