## Turning subtitles on

Open the subtitle button in the player:

- **Default and Video.js players** - a captions (CC) button sits right in the control bar.
- **Shaka player** - subtitles live in the overflow menu.

Pick a language to turn subtitles on, or pick **Off** to hide them again. Languages are shown in your app's language, and variants like hearing-impaired tracks appear with a note, for example "English (SDH)".

## Where it works

| File | Subtitles play on |
|---|---|
| MP4 movies and episodes | Every platform - desktop, Android, and web. |
| MKV movies and episodes | The desktop app (Windows / macOS / Linux). |

On Android and the web, the built-in player can't read subtitles out of an MKV file. Open the stream in an external player instead - see [External players](./external-players/).

## What to expect

- The subtitle list appears a few seconds after playback starts - the app has to look inside the file first.
- Once you pick a track, lines show up within a moment and keep filling in as you watch.
- Not every file has subtitles. The button only shows up when there's something to pick.

<Callout type="note">
  Image-based subtitle formats, like the ones Blu-ray discs use, aren't supported - only text-based tracks.
</Callout>
